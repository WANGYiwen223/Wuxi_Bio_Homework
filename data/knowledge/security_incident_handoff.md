# Suspected account compromise

Article: KB-014 | Service: okta | Version: 1.0

Fictional demo procedure; no real vendor or company authorization is implied.

## Applicability

Suspicious login or potential data breach.

## Prerequisites

Use session-bound identity, current scoped evidence, and backend policy. Never collect passwords or OTPs.

## Procedure

1. Do not request secrets or execute speculative remediation.
2. Capture minimal employee-visible symptoms and urgency.
3. Create a security handoff with available evidence; do not claim containment.

## Verification

Security specialist confirms outcome.

## Escalation

Immediate security specialist handling.
