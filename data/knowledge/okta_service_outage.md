# Identity service outage

Article: KB-003 | Service: okta | Version: 1.0

Fictional demo procedure; no real vendor or company authorization is implied.

## Applicability

Multiple users affected.

## Prerequisites

Use session-bound identity, current scoped evidence, and backend policy. Never collect passwords or OTPs.

## Procedure

1. Check service status and affected scope.
2. Explain the incident with timestamp and do not repeatedly reset credentials.
3. Escalate urgent business impact with the incident reference.

## Verification

Service restored and employee can log in.

## Escalation

Urgent impact or unknown outage scope.
