# Ambiguous IT issue triage

Article: KB-013 | Service: okta | Version: 1.0

Fictional demo procedure; no real vendor or company authorization is implied.

## Applicability

Employee reports a vague problem.

## Prerequisites

Use session-bound identity, current scoped evidence, and backend policy. Never collect passwords or OTPs.

## Procedure

1. Ask which application and what observable error occurred.
2. Ask onset and impact without requesting credentials.
3. Use relevant tools after clarification; stop when investigation budget is exhausted.

## Verification

Issue-specific documented outcome.

## Escalation

No sufficient evidence or missing runbook.
