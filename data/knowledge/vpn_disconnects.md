# Recurring VPN disconnects

Article: KB-004 | Service: vpn | Version: 1.0

Fictional demo procedure; no real vendor or company authorization is implied.

## Applicability

Remote device disconnects every 10–15 minutes.

## Prerequisites

Use session-bound identity, current scoped evidence, and backend policy. Never collect passwords or OTPs.

## Procedure

1. Ask about location, error text, and whether colleagues are affected.
2. Compare service status and assigned-device diagnostic snapshot.
3. Use the stale-profile procedure only if profile mismatch is observed and no applicable outage exists.

## Verification

Stable connection confirmed by employee.

## Escalation

No evidence-supported fix or repeated failure.
