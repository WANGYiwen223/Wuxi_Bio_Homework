# Salesforce regional latency

Article: KB-007 | Service: salesforce | Version: 1.0

Fictional demo procedure; no real vendor or company authorization is implied.

## Applicability

Slow loading shared by colleagues.

## Prerequisites

Use session-bound identity, current scoped evidence, and backend policy. Never collect passwords or OTPs.

## Procedure

1. Ask for office and onset time.
2. Check regional health and recent changes.
3. Report known incident evidence; do not infer a local browser cause without evidence.

## Verification

Employee confirms normal loading after restoration.

## Escalation

Persistent or widespread unresolved latency.
